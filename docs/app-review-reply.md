## Screen recording checklist (James, on a physical iPhone)

Record with Control Centre → Screen Recording, on the latest iOS. Delete the app and reinstall
the review build (TestFlight) first, so the first-launch prompts appear. Sign in to a
**Sandbox** Apple Account (Settings → Developer → Sandbox Apple Account) so the purchase can be
shown without being charged. Aim for 2–4 minutes; trim the race if it's long.

1. Launch the app from the home screen icon (the recording must start here).
2. The consent / tracking prompts on first launch (Google's consent form in the UK, then Apple's
   App Tracking Transparency prompt). Choose either answer.
3. Home screen: scroll to show the banner ad area, the version line and the Privacy policy link;
   open the policy and tap **← Back to the game**.
4. Tap **Go to the garage**. Show the series tabs, the track list, upgrades and paint.
5. Pick a track and press **Race!**. Turn the phone sideways, race a lap or two (steer, drift,
   nitro), finish.
6. Results screen: prize, drift bonus, badges, and the optional **Watch an ad to double it**
   button (tap it if an ad loads).
7. Tap **Bonus: Farmyard Frenzy**, play the one-minute round, see the results.
8. Back in the garage: open the 🏆 Trophy Cabinet (trophies and badges).
9. Paid content: tap the 🪙 **Gold** counter at the top to open the store, buy the smallest Gold
   pack with the Sandbox account, then spend Gold in the garage on a premium paint or a hat.
   Show **Restore purchases** in the store.
10. Open the 🌾 Hay wallet: daily bonus and the optional reward video.

No account registration, login or deletion to show (there are no accounts), and no
user-generated content.

## Reply to paste

Hello, and thank you for reviewing Farmyard Rally. The screen recording is attached, recorded
on an iPhone running the latest iOS, starting from launching the app.

**1. Screen recording**
Attached. It starts on the iPhone home screen and shows launching the app, the home screen and
privacy policy, the garage, a full race, the results screen, the Farmyard Frenzy bonus round,
the trophy and badge cabinet, the wallet, and an In-App Purchase made with a Sandbox account
(buying a Pocket of Gold), plus Restore purchases. The ad consent form (UK/EEA) and Apple's App
Tracking Transparency prompt appear once, on the first launch after installing, so they are not
in this recording. The app has no accounts, no login and no user-generated content.

**2. Purpose and audience**
Farmyard Rally is a casual top-down arcade racing game for a general audience who enjoy short,
light-hearted games. Players race tractors, quads and motorbikes against three computer
rivals on 19 cartoon farm tracks with jumps, mud, hills and bridges. Races last about a minute,
so it suits quick sessions. Winning earns Hay, a free in-game play currency used to upgrade the
vehicle. After each race there is a one-minute bonus round, and there are trophies, badges and
personal bests to aim for. Its value is entertainment: a quick, replayable racing game.

**3. Setup and access**
No login, setup or sample files are needed. Open the app, tap "Go to the garage", choose a
track and press "Race!". The game asks you to turn the phone sideways to race. Steering uses
the ◀ ▶ buttons at the bottom left; DRIFT, NITRO and BRAKE are at the bottom right. The pause
button (top right) has a Controls page. Tracks and the Quad and Motorbike series unlock by
finishing in the top three. The trophy counter in the garage opens the trophy and badge
cabinet.

**4. External services**
- Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for the home-screen banner and optional reward videos,
  with Google's User Messaging Platform for ad consent where the law requires it.
- Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit) for Gold packs and Remove ads.
- Apple App Tracking Transparency, to ask permission before personalised ads.
There is no server or backend of our own, no accounts and no AI services. All game progress is
stored only on the device.

**5. Regional differences**
The game's features and content are the same in every region. The only difference is the ad
consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform), which appears on first launch in regions that
require it, such as the UK, EEA and Switzerland; elsewhere it is not shown. Prices are the App
Store's local prices. The app is in English.

**6. Regulated industries and third-party material**
Not applicable. The app is not in a regulated industry, involves no real-money gambling and
contains no licensed or third-party protected material. All artwork, tracks, names and code are
our own. The in-game currency (Hay) is free play money: it cannot be bought with real money,
sold, withdrawn or exchanged for anything of value.

**7. In-App Purchase**
Tap the 🪙 Gold counter at the top of the screen (on the home screen or in the garage) to open
the store. It offers:
- Pocket of Gold: 100 Gold (consumable)
- Pail of Gold: 500 Gold (consumable)
- Barrow of Gold: 1,000 Gold (consumable)
- Remove ads: hides the home-screen banner (non-consumable)

"Restore purchases" is below the products. Gold is spent in the garage on optional extras:
premium paint jobs, decals, hats and trails, buying an upgrade level with Gold instead of Hay,
and spare nitros. Everything in the game can also be earned by playing; nothing requires a
purchase. Reward videos are always optional and the player chooses to watch them.

Thank you again. Please let us know if you need anything else.
