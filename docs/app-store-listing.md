## Product page (English (U.K.))

**Name:** Farmyard Rally

**Subtitle** (30 max): Muddy tractor racing

**Promotional text** (170 max):
Race tractors, quads and motorbikes round 19 muddy farm tracks. Hit the jumps, drift the bends,
pop the escaped animals and upgrade your ride between races.

**Description** (4,000 max):

Farmyard Rally is a top-down racing game set on a muddy farm. Four racers, one screen, and a
lot of mud.

RACE
• 19 tracks, from Muddy Meadow and Haystack Hill to the hilly Back Forty pack, with jumps, mud,
  water splashes, rumble strips and a real figure-of-eight crossroads.
• Hills you can feel: climbing costs speed, the way down gives it back, and cresting a hill at
  speed throws you into the air.
• Drift round the bends, fire your nitro, grab cash bags and nitro cans before your rivals do.
• Three series: the Tractor Cup, the Quad Cup and the Motorbike Cup, each faster and wilder than
  the last.
• Two cameras: the whole track on one screen, or a close-up that follows you.

PLAY BETWEEN RACES
• Farmyard Frenzy: after every race, 100 escaped animals and one minute to pop as many as you
  can.
• Badges, trophies, fastest laps and personal bests on every track.

UPGRADE AND CUSTOMISE
• Spend the Hay you win on acceleration, top speed, handling and boosts.
• Paint jobs, decals, hats and trails to make your ride your own.

Hay is free in-game play money. It can't be bought, sold or withdrawn and has no cash value.
Optional in-app purchases buy Gold, which
is spent in the garage on cosmetics and optional shortcuts, and Remove ads.

## App Privacy

Privacy policy: the URL above. Game data stays on the device; purchases are handled by Apple.
Data collected comes from the Google Mobile Ads SDK (per Google's data-disclosure guidance):

| Data type | Purposes | Linked to user | Tracking |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Identifiers → Device ID | Third-party advertising, Analytics | Yes | Yes |
| Usage Data → Advertising Data | Third-party advertising, Analytics | No | Yes |
| Usage Data → Product Interaction | Third-party advertising, Analytics | No | No |
| Location → Coarse Location (from IP) | Third-party advertising | No | No |
| Diagnostics → Crash Data | App functionality | No | No |
| Diagnostics → Performance Data | Third-party advertising, Analytics | Yes | No |

"Tracking" is Yes because personalised ads are shown when the player allows tracking at Apple's
prompt.

## App Review information

- **Sign-in required:** No (there are no accounts).
- **Contact:** _(from James)_
- **Notes:**

  Farmyard Rally needs no login. Tap "Go to the garage" on the home screen, pick a track and
  press Race. Turn the phone sideways to race.

  Hay is free play money with no cash value; it can't be bought with real money, sold or
  withdrawn. The three casino-style mini games (Hay Bale Derby, Piggy Bank Slots, Egg Roulette)
  use Hay only, so no real money is ever wagered.

  In-app purchases: tap the 🪙 Gold counter at the top of any screen to open the store (three
  Gold packs and Remove ads), with Restore purchases underneath. Gold is spent in the garage on
  paint jobs, decals, hats and trails, and optional upgrade shortcuts.

  Ads: Google AdMob, a home-screen banner and optional reward videos (wallet, and after a race to
  double the prize). Our AdMob account is newly approved/pending, so ads may not fill during
  review. The app asks for consent (UK/EEA) and App Tracking Transparency permission on first
  launch; declining still shows non-personalised ads.

- **Release:** Manually release this version.
