Farmyard Rally – Privacy policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

Farmyard Rally is a cartoon racing game for iPhone and the web. This page explains what data the game uses and why. In short: your game progress stays on your device, ads are provided by Google AdMob, and purchases go through Apple.

Your game progress

Your Hay and Gold balances, upgrades, paint jobs, trophies, settings and purchases you've made are saved only on your device. There are no accounts and nothing is sent to us. Deleting the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser) deletes it.

Ads (iPhone app)

The iPhone app shows a banner on the home page and offers optional reward videos. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type and how you interact with ads.

  • UK, EEA and Switzerland: on first launch you're asked whether you consent to personalised ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time with Ad privacy choices on the home screen. If you don't consent, no personalised ads are shown.
  • Tracking: the app asks Apple's permission to track before using your advertising identifier. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, you'll only see non-personalised ads. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for a general and family audience.

How Google uses this data is described in Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

The web version shows no third-party ads.

Purchases

Gold packs and Remove ads are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details. The game only learns which item was bought, so it can give it to you.

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads are limited to family-suitable content.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the game's GitHub page.

Changes
